Lootava · mobile game

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Developer: BDATLAS LLC · Contact: support@lootava.com

Lootava is a game where you build an underground shelter and fight Core Duels against other players and bots. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who processes it for us, and how you can delete it.

Short version: we collect what the game needs to run (a player ID, your player name and your saved progress). Gameplay statistics are shared only if you agree. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone. We do not ask for your real name; your player name is whatever you choose.

Your choice about gameplay statistics

After you choose your name, the game asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay data. If you tap NO THANKS, no gameplay statistics are sent. You can change this at any time in the game settings under DATA SHARING.

Services that process data for us

Lootava uses these providers to run the game. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services): accounts and player names, cloud saves, the leaderboard, crash reports, Unity Player Accounts sign-in, and gameplay statistics when you agree. Unity privacy policy
  • Exit Games (Photon): online multiplayer connections. Photon privacy policy
  • Google and Cloudflare: when the game starts it asks one of their public web servers for the current time (so the daily gift cannot be claimed twice by changing the phone clock). Only a standard web request is sent, which includes your IP address; no game data.
  • Google or Apple: only if you sign in with them through LINK ACCOUNT, under their own policies.

These providers may store data on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union.

Purchases and ads

Lootava has an in-game premium currency called Cores, used for cosmetic items, the Season Pass and time skips. It never buys an advantage in duels. When real purchases are enabled, payments are handled by the app store (Google Play or the App Store). We never receive your card details; we receive only a confirmation of what was bought.

The game may offer optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a small in-game reward. Ads never play on their own. When real ads are added, the ad provider may use your device's advertising ID; we will update this policy and name the provider before that happens.

How long we keep data

We keep your account, player name and saved progress until you delete your account. Gameplay statistics are kept by Unity under its retention rules and are not linked to your player name in our reports.

Delete your account and data

In the game

  1. Open your shelter and tap your profile badge in the top-right corner.
  2. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. Type DELETE and tap DELETE FOREVER. You need an internet connection.

This immediately deletes your account, player name, cloud save and the progress stored on your phone. It cannot be undone.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email support@lootava.com with the subject “Delete my Lootava account” and your full player name (for example Rex#4821). We will delete the account and its data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Gameplay statistics already sent cannot be linked back to you after your account is deleted; Unity removes them according to its own rules.

Children

Lootava is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to how it is used. To use these rights, email support@lootava.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

Data sent between the game and our providers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we use well-known providers and collect only what the game needs.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above, and for important changes we will tell you in the game.
