What’s new since build 0.2.0

All in the current beta build.

  • SURGE: 12 character super powers with their own art and effects, host-authoritative online sync.
  • Fighter cards: Common, Rare and Epic rarity, levels 1 to 5, XP from base work, expeditions and duels.
  • Online Vault Raids: real rival vaults, server-side replay check, 6-hour shields, Intruders list and 24-hour revenge.

  • iPhone: cloud-built iOS releases on TestFlight; Android and iPhone in the same matches.

At a glance

Three loops that feed each other: fast duels, raids on real players, and a home players keep for months.

Core Duel + SURGE

1v1, 60 seconds; a tie goes to LAST CHANCE. Shoot a glowing Core into the rival’s Reactor. Power-up boxes and each fighter’s SURGE in every match. No health, no damage.

Vault Raids

One-tap platformer raids on vaults built by real players, checked on the server. Build and test your own trap room; every fall in it pays you.

Underground base

Story intro, then Rex guides new players step by step. Dig floor by floor, upgrade rooms with builders, send expeditions, follow signals to new survivors.

12 fighters, 4 bosses

All 12 survivors playable, each with a SURGE power, a card with rarity and levels, and a cinematic reveal. 3 Guardians and The Warden as bosses.

Monetization

Free to play, one premium currency (Cores): time skips, lockpick refills, fighter XP boosts, Season Pass, opt-in rewarded ads. Weapons and power-up boxes are equal for everyone.

Live-ops ready

Accounts and cloud save, server-side economy (Unity Cloud Code), world leaderboard, Trophy Road, seasons, daily quests, reminder notifications, analytics.
