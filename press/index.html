At a glance

Two games in one loop: short matches and a home players keep for months.

Core Duel + power-ups

1v1, 60–90 seconds. Shoot a glowing Core into the rival’s Reactor. Freeze, Big Core and Shield drop in every match; shoot the box to win them. No health, no damage.

Underground base, guided by Rex

A story intro, then Rex shows new players the base step by step. Dig floor by floor, run rooms, send expeditions, rescue survivors.

12 playable fighters

All 12 survivors implemented and tested, plus 3 Guardians and The Warden as bosses. More every season.

Cross-platform PvP

Android vs iPhone online, quick match and room codes, built on Photon Fusion 2.

Fair monetization

Outfits and Core trails with daily featured picks, Season Pass, time skips, opt-in ads. Money never buys victory.

Accounts and cloud save, leaderboard, shop, seasons, daily gifts, analytics.
