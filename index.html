What is Lootava?

A free-to-play mobile game that mixes shelter building with short, skill-based duels. Real money never buys power in the arena: the premium currency is only for cosmetics, the Season Pass and time skips.

01

Build your shelter

Dig deeper, build rooms, and run your shelter with the survivors you find along the way.

02

Send expeditions

Send your crew into the wasteland for scrap, rare parts and blueprints — the longer the trip, the bigger the risk.

03

Win the Core Duel

Fast 1v1 matches: shoot the Core into the enemy reactor. Play online, climb ten arenas and the world leaderboard.
