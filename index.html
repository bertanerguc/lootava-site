Gameplay

See it in 50 seconds

Straight from the current build: Rex’s intro, the guided base, a Core Duel, a new survivor arriving and the daily rewards.

It starts with Rex

A lone scavenger. A forgotten hatch.

Every player begins with Rex. He crosses the wasteland, finds an old bunker and climbs down. From there he becomes your guide, showing you the base step by step and taking you into your first duel.

Core Duel

Shoot the Core into their Reactor

Your shots never hit the other player, only the glowing Core. Push it into the rival’s Reactor to score a CORE STRIKE. No health bars, no damage.

Four weapons

Pistol, Shotgun, Sniper or Magnet. Each one moves the Core in its own way.

Short and readable

Most strikes in 60–90 seconds wins. A tie goes to LAST CHANCE.

Pick your fighter first

Choose who you play before the duel starts, from the survivors you have found.

New · Power-ups in every match

Shoot the box. Take the power.

A power-up box drops into the arena. Every hit fills its ring, and whoever lands the last shot wins it. So every shot is a choice: the Core, or the box?

Freeze

Your rival is frozen in ice for a few seconds.

Big Core

The Core grows: easier to hit, harder to stop.

Shield

Your Reactor blocks the next strike.

Same for everyone. Power-ups are won only by playing. They are never sold.
